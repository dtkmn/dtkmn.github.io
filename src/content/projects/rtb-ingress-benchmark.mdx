## What it is

An RTB bid-receiver harness compares Quarkus JVM/native, Go, Rust, Python, Spring, and Node under
bursty workloads where latency matters. It separates receiver cost from end-to-end delivery bottlenecks.

## Workload and controls

`http-only` isolates receiver handling; `enqueue` and `confirm` measure different Kafka delivery paths.
Workload shape, resource controls, and mode must be comparable before interpreting a runtime result.
The repository includes side-by-side receivers, load-test automation, observability, and Helm deployment assets.

## Reading the results

The original Quarkus native versus Go article is a historical snapshot. The newer benchmark essay
explains the broader harness and bottleneck separation. A result needs its mode and controls attached;
one throughput chart does not establish a general runtime winner.
